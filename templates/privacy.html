Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register, along with the expense data you log — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

  • Account information (name, email, password)
  • Expense records you create, edit, or delete
  • Basic usage information needed to operate the Service

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide solely to operate and improve Spendly — authenticating your account, storing your expenses, and generating the summaries and breakdowns you see in the app. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in Spendly's database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which your data will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. We may rely on trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting or email delivery services) solely to operate the Service, and any such providers are bound to handle your data securely and only for that purpose.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
